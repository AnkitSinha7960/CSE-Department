About the Department

Established with a vision to foster technical excellence, the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to blend strong theoretical foundations with hands-on industry skills. Our students actively engage in research, hackathons, and cloud-based software development.
